# Information Security Standard

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days for service accounts only.
Personal accounts are not forced to rotate unless a compromise is suspected.

## Incident reporting

Security incidents must be reported to the SOC within one hour of discovery via the #sec-incident channel.

## Laptops

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption. USB mass storage is blocked by default.
